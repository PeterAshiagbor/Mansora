This records a human review. Software setup indicators and an active client
status do not certify the items below. Use a separate training business/device

## Business setup

- [ ] Business name, contact details and receipt footer checked with the owner.
- [ ] Products use the intended individual selling units; carton conversions,
      selling prices and recorded costs checked on representative items.
- [ ] Physical opening stock compared with recorded quantities; discrepancies
      corrected through a recorded stock count or adjustment.
- [ ] Each staff member has their own login. Discount, credit, refund, cost-price
      and shift-review permissions checked with the owner.
- [ ] Owner has reviewed the optional discount ceiling and negative-stock
      setting. Record the owner's choice separately; do not assume a value.

## Daily work and evidence

- [ ] Scanner and actual receipt printer tested on this device.
- [ ] Cash, mobile money/card, split payment and authorised credit rehearsed in
      the training business; staff understand the app records external payment
      amounts but does not collect or verify those payments.
- [ ] Partial refund, credit repayment, receiving, damaged stock and physical
      count rehearsed. Staff understand completed financial records are retained.
- [ ] Till opening, cash movements, till-paid expenses, close variance and
      independent sign-off rehearsed. Actual cash is counted before entry.
- [ ] Owner understands sales, collections, current balances, catalogue margin
      and realised gross profit, including the scope labels on reports.
- [ ] Staff can use page help, retry a failed read, retain a basket on failed
      payment and check Sales history before retrying an uncertain transaction.
- [ ] Staff understand unsubmitted entries are not completed records. Navigation
      warnings protect ordinary exits; restart or session expiry is not draft
      recovery.

## Protection and support

- [ ] Backup created and verified; a separate copy stored away from the laptop.
- [ ] Passphrase stored separately and privately by the owner.
- [ ] Restore rehearsed on a replacement device with synthetic records.
- [ ] Last successful sync, waiting/rejected changes and any incomplete cloud
      history reviewed. The owner understands sync and backup are different.
- [ ] Named support contact and escalation route provided and tested.
- [ ] Training, service plan, commercial terms, hosting/privacy review and
      required signing/release approvals confirmed by their responsible owners.
