The question for the team

What should Mansora Sales & Stock cost - and what must justify that price?

Basic POS and stock tracking are already cheap in Ghana. The price case for Mansora is stronger when we sell the owner-control layer: fewer blind spots around cash, credit, Mobile Money, staff activity, stock movement and day-end reporting.

Pricing signal

Public Ghana-focused POS entry plans commonly sit around GH₵90-150/month. Fuller operating tiers often move into GH₵150-300+, while premium products can reach GH₵299-599+.

That makes the earlier GH₵129 / 229 / 399 idea credible - but the top tier needs enough real value to support the premium.

What customers are really paying us to solve

  • Continuity: the till keeps selling offline and syncs later.
  • Accountability: named logins, audit history, discounts, refunds and shift cash differences make mistakes and theft easier to spot.
  • Credit control: who owes what, repayments, customer statements and reviewed WhatsApp debt reminders.
  • Money checks: Mobile Money/card closing checks plus bank and MoMo statement import in the launch scope.
  • Stock control: low-stock alerts, supplier reorder lists, purchases, adjustments and phone-camera counts.
  • Owner visibility: daily summary email, sales/payment/stock/expense/gross-profit reports, backups and full exports.

What is not yet a strong reason to charge more

Several competitors already offer selling, inventory, basic reports and offline mode at low prices. We should not position Mansora as “just another POS”.

  • Multiple tills / branches: coming soon.
  • VAT receipts: coming soon.
  • Automatic Mobile Money debits: not part of the offer; MoMo is pay-per-invoice.
  • Silent receipt printing in the Windows app: coming soon.

Implication: do not let the Plus price depend mainly on features that are still coming.

Why Mansora can sit above the cheapest tools

The strongest difference is not the checkout screen. It is the combination of named accountability, shift cash checks, credit control, supplier/reorder workflow, owner summaries, MoMo checks, statement import, audit history and assisted local onboarding. That is a clearer business-control story than “POS + stock”.

Why we should not start too high

A shop owner can already find credible Ghana-focused systems below GH₵150. Mansora must first earn proof through pilots, references and reliable support. The Business plan should feel like the obvious upgrade from a notebook - not a software luxury.

Recommended direction

Price for adoption, but protect the value of local support.

Mansora should sit above the cheapest “just sell and track stock” tools, but below a full ERP-style promise until branches and VAT receipts are live.

Balanced launch model

Keep Starter at GH₵129 and Business at GH₵229. These sit inside the live market while giving Mansora room to provide onboarding and human support. Keep Plus at GH₵399 as the future premium anchor, but do not make it the main selling plan until multi-till/branch and VAT receipt features are ready.

Lead with BusinessIt contains the features that most directly answer the owner’s pain: staff accountability, credit, supplier/reorder workflows, reporting, daily summary and MoMo checking.

How it should be sold

1Find the leakAsk about cash shortages, credit, MoMo mismatches and missing stock.
2Demo the controlShow shift differences, credit balances, reorder lists and owner reports.
3Set it upImport the product list, configure staff and train the team.
4Default to BusinessUse Starter as the price-sensitive fallback, not the hero plan.
5Retain locallyMonthly support, easy payment and an owner who knows who to call.

Billing: card can be automatic; Mobile Money is paid per invoice. Keep yearly billing at 2 months free - a familiar market incentive.

Setup fee: keep it, but sell the service

A naked GH₵400 “setup fee” can create friction because several competitors advertise no setup fee, free trials or free data import.

→
Rename it: Assisted onboarding & training - GH₵400.
→
Include: product import, staff setup, permissions, training and first closing check.
→
Waive it: for pilot shops and consider waiving it on annual Business/Plus.
→
Do not compete on free forever: Mansora’s model depends on support and dependable operations, not only software access.

Decision to make now

Recommended: approve GH₵129 Starter and GH₵229 Business for launch; keep GH₵399 Plus as the premium roadmap price; keep 2 months free annually; keep GH₵400 assisted onboarding but waive it for pilots.

What would make us revisit price?

After the pilot, review: close rate, support time per shop, willingness to pay for Business, how often customers ask for branches/VAT, churn risk, and whether the system is preventing enough day-to-day loss or admin work to make the subscription feel obvious.
