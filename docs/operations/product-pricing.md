# Product pricing, carton costs and discounts

On Products, choose **Upload Excel / CSV** to open the existing preview-and-import workflow. Download the Excel template or use your own `.xlsx`/`.csv`. Only the first Excel sheet is read; the file limit is 10 MB. Match the columns and review errors, duplicates, unit costs, selling prices and gross margins before importing. Opening stock needs a separate physical-quantity confirmation. Existing products are skipped, not overwritten; the same file cannot be committed twice.

For each product, either enter **Cost price** per selling unit, or fill both **Carton cost** (GHS) and **Units per carton**. Leave Cost price blank for automatic calculation. If both costing methods are supplied, they must agree after rounding; a conflict blocks import. Quantities must be whole numbers. Selling price and opening stock always refer to the selling unit. Use 1 unit per carton if the product is sold as a whole carton.

The product form also has a carton calculator: enter purchase cost and selling units, review the live result, then choose **Use calculated unit cost**. This sets the catalogue cost only; save the product to persist it. Carton inputs are a calculator, not stored pack definitions, and do not create purchases or add stock. Record subsequent receipts under Purchases & suppliers.

Cost = carton purchase cost ÷ selling units per carton, rounded half up to the nearest pesewa using integer arithmetic. For example, GHS 100 / 24 yields GHS 4.17 per unit. Multiplying rounded unit costs back by the carton quantity can differ from the original carton total; this calculator does not allocate invoice remainders. Purchases continue to use the existing unit-cost and moving-average workflow. Editing catalogue cost does not revalue stock already on hand.

The Products table, exports, product form and import preview show **gross margin**: (selling price − catalogue unit cost) ÷ selling price. This is a percentage of revenue, before discounts and expenses, not markup or realized net profit. A zero selling price shows a dash; below-cost pricing shows a negative percentage. Margin visibility follows **View and edit cost prices**, since a margin reveals cost. Realized sales profit continues to use snapshotted stock costs in Reports.

Owners control **Apply discounts** per staff member under Staff. Default cashiers cannot apply discounts: Checkout shows **Request discount**, and the sale requires credentials from an authorised approver at payment. A request alone creates no sale. Staff who hold that permission can apply discounts directly within the owner's configured limit. The owner can set **Discount limit (% of the basket)** in Settings; above it only the owner can approve. The comparison uses exact integers, including just above the boundary. Zero means no percentage ceiling; permission checks still apply. No percentage policy has been selected on the owner's behalf.

Failed approver passwords are throttled even when a sale is rejected and rolled back: five failures lock that account for one minute. The rejected transaction leaves sale, stock, audit and outbox records unchanged. Approved discounts record the authorising staff ID and discount in the sale audit trail. Disabling a staff account or removing its permission also prevents approval at the engine boundary, independent of the screen.

Validation uses encrypted engine integration tests and Chromium with the real desktop engine and local API. Hardware, Windows screen readers and production deployment are not certified by these checks.
