# Owner guide

## Your daily view

**Dashboard** shows today's and this month's sales, how customers paid, what is
running low and what customers owe you.

## Products and stock

- **Products**: add or edit items, prices, barcodes and reorder levels. Scan a barcode into the Barcode box with a scanner or the **Camera** button.
- **Setting up a scanner or receipt printer**: follow [scanners-and-printers.md](scanners-and-printers.md).
- **Moving from Excel, QuickBooks or Xero**: follow [moving-to-mansora.md](moving-to-mansora.md).
- **Purchases, restocking, suppliers and expenses**: step by step in [stock-purchases-expenses.md](stock-purchases-expenses.md). **Purchases & suppliers → Needs reordering** lists what to order, by supplier.
- Stock is never typed over. It changes through:
  - **Purchases & suppliers → Receive stock** when goods arrive (this also updates your average cost);
  - **Stock → Adjust** for damaged or lost items, with a reason;
  - **Stock → Stock count** after a physical count. Each scan adds one to that product's count.
- Selling is blocked when the system shows no stock. If that stops a real sale, receive or count the stock first.

## Discounts

Under **Settings → Discount limit** you can set the largest discount (as a percentage of the basket) your staff may give. Above it, only you can approve, either by selling yourself or by typing your username and password at the till. Leave it at 0 for no limit.

## Staff

**Sending sign-in details by email.** When you add a staff member you can enter their email address instead of a password and tick *Email them a one-time code*. They receive their username and a one-time code, valid for 48 hours. On the sign-in screen they choose *I have a sign-in code* and set their own password, so you never need to know it. To send a fresh code later, use *Email sign-in code* on the Staff page; a new code replaces the old one. Mansora never emails anyone's password: passwords are stored in a form that cannot be read back. The email address stays on this device and is used for nothing else. Sending needs an internet connection; offline, set a password yourself and tell them in person.

**Staff → Add staff member.** Give every person their own login. Tick what each
person may do. Disable a login the day someone leaves; it takes effect at once.

## Money

- **Cashier shifts**: see every shift's expected cash, counted cash and difference. Sign off closed shifts. The dashboard lists open shifts and flags any left open from an earlier day.
- **Who can do what**: on the Staff page, open *Who can do what* for each role's permissions, or read [who-can-do-what.md](who-can-do-what.md).
- **Credit & repayments**: who owes what, what is overdue, and record repayments. A repayment is never counted as a new sale.
- **Expenses**: record running costs. They are shown separately from the cost of goods.

## Reports

**Daily summary email.** In Settings, *Daily summary email*, enter an address and the time to send after, then choose *Turn on*. Once a day, after that time and when the till is online, you get one email with net sales, payments taken, refunds, discounts, any cash shortages at shift close, low stock and customer credit owed. Costs and profit are never included. It travels through Mansora's email service, which does not keep the figures. Use *Send today's summary now* to check it, or *Turn off* to stop.

**Mobile money & card check** (Reports): at closing, compare the expected mobile money and card totals with your wallet statement and card terminal report. Every payment is listed with its transaction ID. A red warning means the same ID was entered twice, which can be a fake or reused payment message; a yellow one means a payment has no ID to match.

Sales, payments, gross profit and stock for any dates, exportable to Excel, CSV
or printed/PDF. Reports work without internet. "Gross profit" is sales minus the
cost of the goods sold; it is not your final profit, because the system is not a
full accounting package.

## Your data and Mansora

- Your data is saved on this laptop first and copied to the Mansora cloud when online.
- **Mansora staff cannot see your sales.** If you want help, go to **Mansora access**, say why and for how long. You can stop it at any time, and you can see every time someone looked.
- **Import & export → Export all business data** gives you a full copy whenever you want.

## Protect yourself against losing the laptop

1. Keep the laptop online when you can, so records reach the cloud.
2. **Backup & recovery → Create encrypted backup** regularly. Copy the file to a USB drive kept somewhere else.
3. Write the backup passphrase down and keep it away from the laptop. Nobody, including Mansora, can open a backup without it.

Records made after your last sync and last backup cannot be recovered if the
laptop is destroyed. The Sync status screen warns you when such records exist.

## Audit history

Every sale, refund, price change, stock adjustment, staff change and refused
action is recorded with who and when. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.

## If a Mansora invoice is overdue

Your till never stops because of an unpaid Mansora invoice. You first see a reminder on the dashboard, then, 15 days after the due date, a warning that names the date reports will pause. If the invoice is still unpaid 30 days after its due date, reports and report exports pause until it is paid. Selling, refunds, shifts, stock, customers, backups, the full business data export and Billing & payments keep working throughout, including when the laptop is offline. Pay from **Billing & payments**; reports reopen at the next sync. If you need more time, ask Mansora for a payment arrangement. If you think an invoice is wrong, tell Mansora: a disputed invoice never pauses anything.
