# Cashier guide

## Starting your day

1. Sign in with your own username and password. This works without internet.
2. Choose **New sale**. Count the cash in the till and enter it as the opening float. **Open shift**.

## Making a sale

1. Scan each item, or type part of its name and click it. Press **F2** to jump back to the search box.
2. Change quantities with **+ / −** or type the number.
3. Press **F9** (or **Pay**).
4. Type the cash you were given. The change is shown in large figures.
   - Mobile money or card: enter the amount on that line. Check the customer's payment has really arrived first — the system only records it.
   - Part cash, part mobile money: fill in both lines.
5. Press **Enter** to complete. Give the change. **Print receipt** if the customer wants one.
6. Press **Enter** for the next customer.

If the receipt does not print, the sale is still saved. Reprint it from **Sales history**.

## Things that need a manager

A discount, a refund, a credit sale and taking cash out of the till ask for a
manager's username and password. Call your manager to type them in.

## Refunds

Sales history → click the receipt → **Refund…** → enter the quantity coming back →
give a reason. Untick "Sellable" if the item is damaged.

## Ending your shift

Cashier shifts → **Close shift** → count all the cash and enter it. The system
shows if the till is short or over. If it does not balance, write why.

## If the internet goes off

Keep selling. The bar at the bottom shows "Offline" and how many records are
waiting. They are sent automatically when the internet returns.

## You cannot

Change prices or costs, delete a sale, see profit reports or manage staff. This
is by design.
