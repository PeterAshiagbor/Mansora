# Mansora Sales & Stock: terms of service

Version: draft 0.1, 2026-11-14. Words in [square brackets] must be filled in before use.

These terms are an agreement between **[Mansora registered company name]**, registration number
[number], of [registered address], Ghana ("**Mansora**", "we") and the business that signs up
("**the client**", "you"). They apply when the client's owner accepts them when activating Mansora, or signs an order form.

## 1. What Mansora provides

1.1 Mansora provides software to record sales, stock, customers, credit, expenses, staff and reports
("**the service**"), in two forms: a browser version reached over the internet, and an offline version
installed on the client's Windows laptop that syncs with Mansora's servers when it is online.

1.2 Mansora provides help by phone, WhatsApp or email during [support hours], as set out in the client's plan.

1.3 Mansora may improve the service. It will not remove a feature the client's plan includes without
30 days' notice, unless the law or security requires it sooner.

## 2. The client's account and staff

2.1 The owner controls the client's account. Each staff member must have their own login. The client must
not share logins, because the records show who did what.

2.2 The client is responsible for what its staff do in the service, for choosing who has which role, and
for keeping the laptop, passwords and the backup passphrase safe.

2.3 The backup passphrase is known only to the client. **Mansora cannot recover it.** If it is lost,
backups made with it cannot be opened.

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 Fees are as stated in the client's plan or order form, in Ghana cedis. [State whether fees include VAT,
NHIL and GETFund, once Mansora's VAT position is confirmed.]

3.2 Mansora sends an invoice before each billing period. Payment is due on the due date on the invoice.
The client may pay by card, Mobile Money or bank transfer, or authorise automatic payment (see
[direct-debit-consent.md](direct-debit-consent.md)). Mansora never holds the client's own sales money.

3.3 Mansora may change its fees with at least 30 days' written notice. The new fee applies from the next
billing period after the notice ends. The client may cancel before then.

3.4 A client who believes an invoice is wrong should tell Mansora before the due date. While Mansora looks
into it, the disputed invoice does not count towards the steps in clause 4.

## 4. If an invoice is not paid

This clause matches how the software works (decision D-28).

4.1 **Selling never stops.** Whatever is owed, the client can always sell, refund, manage stock, customers and
shifts, sync, make backups, export all of its business data, and pay Mansora.

4.2 The steps, counted from the invoice's due date:
- from day [1]: a notice in the app and by email;
- from day [15]: a warning that names the date reports will pause;
- from day [30]: management reports and report exports pause until the invoice is paid or an arrangement is agreed.
Mansora will never pause reports sooner than 21 days after the due date, or with less than 7 days' warning.

4.3 The client can ask for a payment arrangement. If Mansora agrees, the steps stop until the arrangement's
end date (up to 60 days at a time).

4.4 If an invoice is still unpaid [90] days after its due date, Mansora may end these terms under clause 9,
after giving the client a final 14 days' written notice and the chance to export its data.

## 5. The client's data

5.1 **The client's business records belong to the client.** These are its sales, stock, customers, staff,
suppliers, expenses and similar records ("**client data**").

5.2 Mansora processes client data only to provide the service, on the client's instructions, as set out in the
[data processing agreement](data-processing-agreement.md), which forms part of these terms.

5.3 Mansora does not sell client data, use it for advertising, or share one client's data (including customer
debts) with another client or with credit bureaus.

5.4 Mansora may use counts that cannot identify any person or the client (for example, the number of sales a day
across all shops) to run and improve the service.

5.5 Mansora staff can see client data only (a) when the owner grants support access in the app, for a limited
time, (b) when needed to fix a fault or security problem, recorded in the audit log, or (c) when the law requires it.

5.6 Client data is stored on servers of Mansora's hosting provider. **At the date of these terms the servers are
in the United States.** See the data processing agreement for safeguards and the list of subprocessors.

## 6. The client's duties under data protection law

6.1 For its customers' and staff's personal data, the client is the data controller under the Data Protection
Act, 2012 (Act 843). The client must register with the Data Protection Commission where the law requires,
tell its customers and staff how their data is used (Mansora provides notice templates), and record only what it needs.

6.2 The client must not record special categories of personal data (for example health details, religion or
criminal records) in free-text fields unless the law allows it and it is necessary.

## 7. Availability and the offline version

7.1 Mansora aims to keep the browser version available [99%] of each month, excluding announced maintenance.
It cannot promise the internet connection in the shop.

7.2 The offline version keeps working without the internet and syncs later. The client should sync and back up
regularly. Data on a laptop that is lost or broken before it syncs or is backed up cannot be recovered by Mansora.

## 8. Responsibility and limits

8.1 Mansora will provide the service with reasonable skill and care. The client remains responsible for its own
tax returns, prices, stock counts, cash and business decisions. Reports help but do not replace an accountant.

8.2 Neither party is responsible for loss caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as power cuts,
network outages or natural events, provided it acts reasonably to limit the effect.

8.3 Except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything else the law does not allow to be
limited, Mansora's total responsibility under these terms in any 12 months is limited to the fees the client paid
in those 12 months. Neither party is responsible for lost profits or indirect loss.

8.4 Nothing in these terms takes away rights the client has under Ghanaian law that cannot be excluded.

## 9. Ending the agreement

9.1 The client may cancel at any time from Billing in the app or by writing to Mansora. Cancellation takes effect
at the end of the paid period. Fees already paid are not refunded, except where Mansora is at fault.

9.2 Mansora may end these terms with 30 days' written notice, or sooner if the client seriously breaks them and does
not fix it within 14 days of being asked, or as set out in clause 4.4.

9.3 **After the end**, the client keeps its offline data on its laptop and can export everything. Mansora keeps
the client's cloud data for [90] days so the client can export it, then deletes or anonymises it, except records the
law requires Mansora to keep (for example its own invoices and payments). See the [retention schedule](retention-schedule.md).

## 10. Confidentiality and intellectual property

10.1 Each party keeps the other's confidential information private and uses it only for these terms.

10.2 Mansora owns the software. The client receives the right to use it while these terms are in force.
The client must not copy, resell or reverse-engineer it, except where the law allows.

## 11. Changes to these terms

Mansora may change these terms by giving 30 days' notice in the app or by email. If the client does not accept
the change, it may cancel before the change takes effect without paying for later periods.

## 12. Law and disputes

12.1 These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

12.2 The parties will first try to settle a dispute by talking, then by mediation under the Alternative Dispute
Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798) [at the Ghana ADR Centre or another agreed centre]. If mediation fails within 30 days,
either party may go to the courts of Ghana [or arbitration under Act 798, if preferred].

## 13. Notices and contact

Notices go to the email address in the client's account and to [Mansora contact email]. Mansora's details:
[registered name, address, phone, email, DPC registration number].
