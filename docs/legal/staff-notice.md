# Notice to shop staff: your login and what is recorded

To be shown to each staff member at their first sign-in, and kept on the shop wall or in the staff file.
The shop fills in the brackets.

---

**[Shop name]** uses Mansora Sales & Stock to record sales and stock. This notice explains what it records about you.

**Your own login.** You have your own username and password. Do not share them, and do not use anyone else's.
Everything done under your login is treated as done by you.

**What is recorded.** Your name, username and role. If you give one, your email, used only to send you sign-in codes.
Each time you sign in and out. The sales, refunds, discounts, payments, cash in and out of the till, stock changes and
price changes you make, with the time. Your shift: opening float, cash counted at close, and any difference.

**Why.** To run the shop, keep the cash and stock correct, find and fix mistakes, and keep records the law requires.

**Who can see it.** The owner, and managers for the parts their role allows. Mansora, the software provider, stores the
records for the shop, and sees reports only if the owner allows it for a short time.

**How it may be used.** The records may be used to check a shortage, a stock loss or a complaint. If they are used in a
disciplinary matter, you will be told what the records show and you can give your explanation before any decision is made.

**How long.** Sales and cash records are kept for [six] years, as business and tax records. Your login is switched off
when you leave; your name stays on the records you made.

**Your rights.** You can ask the owner to see the records about you, and to correct anything wrong. You can complain to
the Data Protection Commission if you think your data is mishandled.
