# DRAFT — not legal advice — for review by a Ghanaian data-protection lawyer

## Questions on Act 843, registration and cross-border processing

Use the [inventory](data-inventory.md), [retention questions](retention-deletion.md), [notice template](privacy-notice.md) and [agreement outline](controller-processor-outline.md) as evidence for this review. Record the lawyer's answer, source/current guidance, decision owner and approval date; do not treat an unanswered question as approval.

1. **Roles and registration.** Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and current Data Protection Commission requirements, which Mansora entity and which shops must register, in which capacity, before what processing begins? Are sole traders and employer/staff records treated differently? What application details, fees, renewal obligations, contacts and evidence apply? Who verifies registration and keeps it current?
2. **Purposes and lawful grounds.** What lawful grounds apply separately to optional customer contacts, credit records, staff activity/approvals, supplier contacts, client billing/onboarding, administrator authentication and security/audit logs? Is consent appropriate for any of these, and how should withdrawal be handled without misleading customers about financial retention? Is an owner support grant a product access control rather than the complete legal basis for every processing activity?
3. **Fair collection and notices.** What notice content, timing, languages and formats are required for customers, workers, suppliers and sole-trader clients? Which fields are genuinely necessary? Should free-text notes, expense/refund reasons, payment/invoice references and import filenames be restricted or trained against unnecessary sensitive content? What extra rules apply if minors' or sensitive/special-category information is entered?
4. **Rights and complaints.** Which access, correction, objection, deletion and complaint rights and deadlines apply? How should identity be checked proportionately? What exceptions/holds and explanations are permitted? How can an individual response be prepared without exposing other customers, employee details, full-business exports or authentication material? Which current Commission complaint details should a final notice use?
5. **Retention and reliable accounts.** Which Ghanaian tax/accounting, employment, credit, limitation and dispute requirements govern each table and copy? What starts the period, and when is a hold required? Can contact/profile linkage be reduced while immutable financial totals/IDs remain? How should historical JSON payloads, immutable audit reasons, support logs and old backup copies be treated? What engineering capability must exist before promising erasure?
6. **Security and operators.** What measures, contracts and evidence are proportionate for offline laptops, OS/device access, staff permissions, cloud database roles/RLS, MFA, encryption keys, backup passphrases, patching, physical loss and privileged infrastructure operators? Who is allowed to issue owner recovery codes or replace/relink a device, and what identity-verification procedure is required? What audits and access reviews are expected?
7. **Hosting map.** Before selecting a provider, where will primary database rows, replicas, encrypted object backups, provider snapshots, logs, optional error reports and support access be located? What countries can provider staff/admins access them from? What controls and commitments can the chosen providers actually evidence? Are encrypted backups still within the relevant transfer/personal-data duties?
8. **Cross-border conditions.** For each proposed country/recipient/remote-access flow, what Act 843 conditions, Commission guidance, contractual provisions, safeguards, transparency, permissions or assessments apply? Are there sector/client-specific restrictions or localisation obligations? Which onward-transfer and subprocessor-change controls are needed? No Ghana-only, approved-country or transfer-lawfulness conclusion is made here.
9. **Processors and agreement.** Which instructions, confidentiality/security obligations, assistance/audit rights, subprocessor permissions, incident clauses, exit/export/deletion provisions and liability terms must the Mansora–client agreement contain? Which activities are Mansora's own controller purposes? How should host/database privileges and independent provider purposes be reflected rather than hidden behind dashboard restrictions?
10. **Incidents.** What events trigger notification duties, to whom and within what applicable time, and who leads investigation and communications? What records should be kept, how long, and what evidence can be shared safely? Does a lost laptop require different handling depending on encryption, OS access, credentials, backups and recent synchronization? Which regulator/subject contact procedures must be ready before deployment?
11. **Operational copies and exit.** Who controls receipts, PDFs, exported Excel/JSON, original import workbooks, removable media and owner-held backups? What practical exit process, deletion evidence and restore-after-redaction safeguards are required? How should persistent offline copies and immutable history limit a final notice's promises?
12. **Approval and maintenance.** Who signs off the final notices, role assignments, registration, retention schedule, host/transfer plan and operational procedures? What product/schema/provider/purpose changes trigger a new review? How is the inventory test made part of release review, and who must stop a release when a new field lacks classification?

### Outstanding evidence

The repository does not establish a production hosting country/provider, current registrations, signed processing contracts, lawful retention periods, approved marketing uses, a full personal-data request/erasure workflow, or actual Windows/NVDA/physical hardware acceptance. Obtain and document the missing facts before presenting any draft as an approved policy or a compliance claim.
