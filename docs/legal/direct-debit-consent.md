# Automatic payment authorisation wording

To be shown on the Billing page before the owner approves automatic payment, and copied into the confirmation email.
Payments are taken by a licensed payment provider (Paystack), so Mansora never holds card details or the client's money,
in line with the Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987).

---

**Authorise automatic payment for Mansora**

By approving, you allow [Mansora registered name] to charge your [card ending ••••] through Paystack for Mansora
invoices on or after their due date.

- **Amount:** each charge is the amount owed on that invoice, never more than **GHS [limit]** per charge.
  A larger amount needs your new approval.
- **When:** on the invoice due date. If a charge fails, Mansora tries at most 3 times in total, 3 days apart.
- **Before each charge:** Mansora emails the invoice at least [7] days before the due date.
- **Cancel any time:** from Billing in the app, or by writing to [email]. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not
  cancel what is already owed.
- **Wrong charge:** tell Mansora within [30] days. If Mansora charged in error, it refunds the amount in full.

[Approve automatic payment]  [Not now]

---

### Notes
- The app already records the method, masked card, limit and approval time for each authorisation, and lets the owner cancel.
  Store the wording version shown, too (task L1 in the compliance register).
- Recurring Mobile Money debits are not offered through Paystack in Ghana; the app says so.
- Confirm with Paystack's terms that this wording meets their merchant requirements for recurring charges.
