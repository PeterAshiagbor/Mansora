# DRAFT — not legal advice — for review by a Ghanaian data-protection lawyer

## Retention and deletion proposal for decision

No periods below are agreed policy and no purge/deletion job is implemented. Which Ghanaian tax/accounting, limitation, employment, credit and data-protection requirements apply to each shop and Mansora, and when should a legal hold stop deletion? The lawyer and each responsible controller must approve purposes, triggers, periods, access and evidence before engineering changes. Current records do not automatically expire just because a business closes, a grant expires, a device is revoked or an account is disabled.

### Cross-system consequences

A deletion decision must cover the live laptop, cloud current rows, immutable financial/audit facts, local outbox, historical cloud sync payloads, support projections, encrypted backup objects, hosting backups, exports/receipts and any operational logs. Offline laptops can replay old snapshots. A workable design needs versioned redaction/tombstones or equivalent suppression, durable sync acknowledgement/deduplication, restore/replay tests, an auditable approval process and a legal-hold mechanism. These are proposed requirements, not shipped deletion capabilities.

Financial records and local audit facts are immutable by design. Current code has no general right-to-erasure endpoint. Do not directly disable triggers, delete history or run a tenant cascade as an improvised privacy procedure. An approved design may minimise/pseudonymise linked profiles while preserving necessary totals/IDs, but whether that is lawful and sufficient needs the lawyer. Backups remain personal-data-bearing ciphertext; expiry needs verified deletion of all relevant copies and a policy for restores, rather than a promise of immediate erasure from offline/owner-controlled media.

### `cloud.demo_requests` (C39)

Proposal for legal/owner review: choose a short retention window for unqualified or closed demo requests, and preserve only the minimum contact-consent evidence needed for a justified period. No automatic deletion period is silently chosen by this release. Qualified leads do not become clients automatically. A deletion procedure must cover the prospect row, linked lead-ID audit metadata where appropriate, and expiry of provider backups; it must not delete unrelated client/financial records. Who may approve and execute a prospect data request, and what retention period applies?
